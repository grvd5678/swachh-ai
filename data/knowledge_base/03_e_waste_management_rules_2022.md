# E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 (MoEFCC, Government of India)
**Source:** Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change Notification G.S.R. 801(E) (November 2, 2022, effective April 1, 2023)
**Jurisdiction:** National (India)
**Scope:** Electrical and Electronic Equipment (EEE), Consumer Electronics, Chargers, IT hardware, Household E-Waste

---

### Rule 3 & Schedule I: Categories of Electrical and Electronic Equipment Covered
- **Information Technology and Telecommunication Equipment:** Cellular phones, chargers, laptops, tablets, printers, cartridges, power adapters, cables, keyboards, mice, monitors.
- **Consumer Electrical and Electronics:** Television sets, radios, microwave ovens, refrigerators, washing machines, audio systems.
- **Lighting Equipment:** Fluorescent lamps, other lighting fixtures.

---

### Rule 5: Responsibilities of Consumers and Bulk Consumers
1. **Mandatory Source Segregation:**
   - Every consumer or citizen generating e-waste shall ensure that e-waste is segregated from ordinary municipal solid waste (wet and dry garbage).
   - E-waste must not be handed over to the informal, unregistered scrap sector (kabadiwalas) if they employ crude, polluting recovery processes (acid leaching, open burning of cables).

2. **Authorized Disposal Pathways:**
   - Consumers must channelize e-waste exclusively through:
     - (a) Registered e-waste collection points / centers established by producers under Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR).
     - (b) Authorized dismantlers or recyclers registered with the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) or State Pollution Control Board (SPCB).
     - (c) Take-back systems or buy-back schemes operated by electronics manufacturers/retailers (e.g., brand exchange programs).
     - (d) Designated e-waste drop-off bins at authorized municipal locations.

3. **Guidance for Cables, Chargers, and Small Accessories:**
   - **Category:** E-waste (Telecommunication & IT accessory).
   - **Handling:** Bundle cords neatly. Do NOT burn plastic-sheathed copper cables to extract wire (releases toxic dioxins and furans).
   - **Disposal Pathway:** Deposit in e-waste bins at electronic retail stores, authorized e-waste collection drives, or municipal e-waste drop boxes.
