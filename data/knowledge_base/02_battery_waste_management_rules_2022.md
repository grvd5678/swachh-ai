# Battery Waste Management Rules, 2022 (MoEFCC, Government of India)
**Source:** Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change Notification G.S.R. 664(E) (August 22, 2022)
**Jurisdiction:** National (India)
**Scope:** Portable Batteries, Lithium-ion Cells, Power Banks, Electric Vehicle Batteries, Consumer Obligations

---

### Rule 3: Definitions and Categorization
- **"Portable Battery":** A battery that is sealed, weighs less than 5 kilograms, not made for industrial or automotive use, and includes consumer batteries used in mobile devices, flashlights, watches, and portable power banks.
- **"Waste Battery":** Any battery that is depleted, broken, damaged, end-of-life, discarded, or intended to be discarded.
- **"Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR)":** Responsibility of producers and manufacturers to ensure environmentally sound management, collection, and recycling of waste batteries.

---

### Rule 4: Responsibilities of Consumers and Waste Generators
1. **Mandatory Separation from Household Waste:**
   - Consumers and waste generators shall discard waste batteries separately from other waste streams (wet waste, dry waste, municipal garbage).
   - Waste batteries must NEVER be dumped in open ground, burned, or disposed of in regular household waste bins.

2. **Handover Protocol for Consumers:**
   - Consumers must surrender or deposit waste batteries (including spent power banks, dry cells, button cells, and lithium-ion packs) exclusively to:
     - (a) Authorized collection centres established by producers/manufacturers under EPR.
     - (b) Retailers or distributors of batteries offering buy-back or take-back schemes.
     - (c) Registered recyclers or refurbishers.
     - (d) Local municipal designated hazardous / e-waste deposition points.

3. **Safety Warnings on Lithium-Ion Packs & Power Banks:**
   - **Fire & Thermal Runaway Risk:** Damaged, swollen, or punctured lithium-ion batteries or power banks pose severe fire and explosion risks if compacted inside municipal waste trucks or exposed to water/heat.
   - **Handling Damaged/Swollen Power Banks:** 
     - Do not puncture, bend, or crush.
     - Isolate the battery: tape the electrical terminals with non-conductive electrical tape.
     - Store in a cool, dry, non-flammable container (e.g., metal can or sand) away from flammable items until taken to an authorized e-waste/battery collection center.
     - Do NOT attempt to charge or use a swollen power bank.
