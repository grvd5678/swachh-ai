# Kolkata Municipal Corporation (KMC) & WBPCB Waste Segregation Guidance
**Source:** Kolkata Municipal Corporation Solid Waste Management Bye-laws & West Bengal Pollution Control Board (WBPCB) Citizen Guidelines
**Jurisdiction:** Kolkata, West Bengal (Local Context)
**Scope:** Ward-level doorstep waste collection, Bin colour coding, Local Domestic Hazardous & E-Waste Pathways

---

### Local Bin System & Segregation Protocols in Kolkata
Kolkata Municipal Corporation (KMC) enforces a segregated collection system at the household and ward level:

1. **Green Bin / Container (Wet / Biodegradable Waste):**
   - Vegetable and fruit peels, food leftovers, tea leaves, eggshells, garden leaves, perishable organic matter.
   - Handed over daily to primary door-to-door municipal collection workers (karyakartas/sanitation staff).

2. **White or Blue Bin / Container (Dry / Recyclable Waste):**
   - Clean plastic packets, milk pouches, PET bottles, cartons, cardboard, dry paper, glass bottles, metal tins.
   - Handed over to door-to-door collection workers during designated dry waste collection schedules or local scrap recyclers.

3. **Domestic Hazardous & Special Waste (Black Bag / Separate Handover):**
   - **Covered Items:** Expired medicines, CFLs and tube lights, used batteries, broken mercury devices, insecticide/chemical spray cans, sanitary waste.
   - **KMC Rule:** Domestic hazardous items must **never** be mixed into green or blue carts.
   - **Collection Method:** Must be kept in a distinct secure bag (marked Hazardous) and handed over separately to the KMC conservancy worker during morning doorstep rounds, or dropped off at the Ward Conservancy Office / Compactor Station.

---

### Local E-Waste & Battery Drop-off Pathways in Kolkata
1. **E-Waste Collection Drives:**
   - WBPCB and KMC organize periodic residential e-waste collection drives in association with CPCB-authorized PROs (Producer Responsibility Organisations) and registered e-waste recyclers.
2. **Electronics Brand & Retail Drop-off:**
   - Large electronic retail hubs (e.g., Chandni Chowk electronics market, authorized retail outlets in Kolkata malls) host designated EPR e-waste return drop-boxes for spent batteries, chargers, and mobile devices.
3. **Citizen Advisory:**
   - Do not hand over lithium-ion power banks or chemical batteries to informal itinerant waste pickers who smash or burn them. Use authorized collection points or handover to KMC special waste staff.
